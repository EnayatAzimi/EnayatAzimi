Network+

Network+ is the foundation of all IT and cybersecurity. It teaches networking basics, protocols, ports, OSI model, routing, switching, and troubleshooting. Without strong networking, hacking is impossible.

Linux

Linux is the heart of cybersecurity. Almost all hacking tools run on Linux, especially Kali, ParrotOS, and Ubuntu Server. Learning terminal commands gives you full control over systems.

Bash Scripting

Bash scripting allows you to automate tasks, build your own tools, scan networks, and manage systems efficiently. Every ethical hacker needs to understand scripting.

Security+

Security+ teaches the fundamentals of cybersecurity: encryption, risks, attacks, security policies, secure network design, and incident response. It's the best defensive-security foundation.

White Hat Hacker

A White Hat Hacker uses hacking skills for good — to protect people, organizations, and systems. Their mission is to stop attackers, secure networks, and report vulnerabilities legally and ethically.

CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)

CEH is one of the most famous hacking certifications. It teaches penetration testing, exploits, reconnaissance, scanning, enumeration, and real-world attack techniques used by both hackers and defenders.
